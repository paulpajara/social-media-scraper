# Notes for documentation and interpretation

The completed reference corpus has **231 posts**: 182 Bluesky and 49 Mastodon, with 104 negative, 110 neutral and 17 positive AI-assisted target-polarity labels. Original acquisition contained 364 raw posts; preparation retained 335 candidates; annotation excluded 96 and left 8 unresolved. The audit preserves every decision. The final corpus covers observed publication dates from February 4 to October 12, 2026, with different coverage by platform. This is an uneven bounded sample, not a full-year population survey.

The third blind AI reviewer checked a simple random sample of 60 candidates. Before adjudication, exact decision agreement was 54/60, relevance agreement 56/60, and conditional sentiment agreement 38/40 for posts both included. Six disagreements and one additional commercial advertisement were adjudicated before the reference corpus was frozen. Shared AI errors remain possible; these consistency figures do not establish human validation.

The fixed lexicon's **24-post holdout** has 45.8% agreement accuracy, macro F1 0.222 and balanced accuracy 0.333 against the AI references. The ten-fold development means are 51.3% accuracy, macro F1 0.312 and balanced accuracy 0.373. These show a weak sentiment baseline, especially for target polarity. The model predicts 200 posts neutral with zero matched cues; that fallback is not evidence of neutral public opinion. Use the supplied reference summaries for describing this annotated corpus, and the evaluation CSVs for assessing the rule model. Some references use public link-preview context that the classifier does not see. The positive class has only 17 records (2 in the holdout), so per-class results are particularly unstable. No sentiment rules or reference labels were changed after inspecting holdout results.

Three LDA topics were fitted after removing the exact repeated publisher footer and emoji-only tokens from topic/proximity features. The source text and sentiment input remain unchanged. Topic NPMI coherence is -0.099, 0.198 and 0.162; top-term diversity is 0.633. Topics overlap and reflect source wording as well as substantive discussion. Topic 0 has weak coherence; publisher branding remains among topic 2's terms. Inspect representative posts before assigning topic names, and discuss these limitations rather than presenting three sharply separated policy themes as established findings. Perplexity and coherence are in-sample diagnostics.

The 5,000 sampled pairs contain cosine, Jaccard and Euclidean proximity measures. They cover a subset of all possible post pairs. Similar text does not establish shared stance, coordination or truthful reporting.
